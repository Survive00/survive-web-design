Our Engineering Philosophy

At Survive Web Design, led by web designer and WordPress developer Muritala Sodiq, we believe that web applications should be blazingly fast, accessible, and structured without unnecessary bloat.

Why Framework-Free Frontend?

Heavy frontend frameworks often introduce massive dependencies and slow loading speeds. By utilizing pure, semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript, we deliver lightweight code that scores exceptionally high on Google PageSpeed Insights.

  • WordPress Custom Themes & Plugin Tuning
